1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how MaxAskezaPL ("we", "us" or "the developer") handles information in Verminside, our Android game distributed through Google Play, including test versions.

The Android application identifier is com.AskezaMaxPL.Verminside. We are responsible for the processing we determine for the game. You can contact us at max.omelchenko.pl@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current version of the game. Reading this policy or continuing to play does not, by itself, constitute consent to processing that requires consent.

2. Information we process

Local game data

The game stores run and board state, route, inventory, in-game currency, achievements, unlocked classes, tutorial progress, language, and sound and interface preferences on your device. This information allows the game to function and restore your progress.

We do not upload these saves to a developer-operated server. The current version does not create a player account or synchronize progress to the cloud. Device or operating system backup services may separately back up application data according to your device settings.

Unity engine diagnostics

The game uses the Unity engine with technical diagnostic reporting enabled. Unity can automatically receive an installation-specific identifier and reports about crashes, errors, unresponsive application events and performance. Reports can include device and operating system information, app and Unity versions, memory usage, timestamps and technical logs. Network requests also expose the IP address needed to deliver them.

We and Unity use this information to identify and fix technical problems and improve compatibility, reliability and performance. Technical diagnostics are separate from advertising or gameplay marketing analytics.

Information you send to support

When you email us, we receive your email address, any name shown by your email service, the message and any attachments you choose to provide, such as a bug report. We use them to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location for gameplay.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

We process information to provide and restore gameplay, diagnose faults, maintain game security and stability, respond to support and privacy requests, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the game or support you request, our legitimate interest in maintaining a functioning and secure game, and compliance with legal obligations. We obtain consent before processing that requires consent under applicable law. You can contact us to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

We do not sell your personal information or use the current game's data for targeted advertising.

4. Services and data recipients

  • Unity: Unity Technologies and its service providers process the technical diagnostics described above. We can access reports relevant to this game. Read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Diagnostics privacy information.
  • Email support: our contact mailbox uses Google's Gmail service. Google and relevant email delivery providers process messages and associated delivery information so we can receive and answer them. Read the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: Google separately processes information related to distributing and managing the app under its own policies. The game does not receive your Google account password or payment card details.

We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims, subject to applicable safeguards.

Advertising, accounts, purchases and future features

The current version has no active AdMob or Unity Ads integration, Google Play Games Services, Firebase Cloud Firestore synchronization, or Unity Analytics gameplay event collection. It does not process real-money purchases through its in-game currency screen.

We plan to consider these services in future versions. Before introducing them, we will update this policy to identify the actual information processed, recipients, purposes, retention and deletion procedures, and provide any required notices and consent choices. This policy does not authorize additional processing simply because a future service is mentioned.

5. Retention and deletion

Local saves and preferences

Local data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or it is removed with the app. To remove it, open Android device settings → Apps → Verminside → Storage → Clear data or Clear storage. The exact labels vary by device. This deletes local saves, settings and permanent progress.

Starting a new run or resetting a run inside the game does not erase permanent progress such as diamonds, achievements and classes. Clearing app data does not remove data already received by Unity or copies held by your device backup provider. Manage those copies using the relevant provider's controls.

Unity diagnostic records

Unity's default retention period for personal diagnostic data is 90 days. You can request access to or deletion of diagnostic records associated with your installation by contacting us. We will coordinate the request with Unity using the available service procedures. Records that cannot reasonably be linked to your installation may not be identifiable for an individual request.

Support correspondence

We retain support messages for as long as necessary to resolve your request and related follow-up, and to satisfy applicable legal obligations or handle a dispute. We delete information when these purposes no longer require it, or following a valid deletion request unless lawful retention is necessary. If we must retain information after your request, we explain the reason and applicable retention period or criteria.

6. Your rights and requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a privacy or deletion request, email max.omelchenko.pl@gmail.com with the subject Verminside — data deletion or Verminside — privacy request. Describe the information or request concerned. If you already have an installation identifier, you may include it; otherwise, we will help determine what information is needed to locate the records.

We may ask only for information reasonably necessary to verify your request and protect other users' data. We do not need your Google password. We respond within the deadlines required by applicable law. For local game data, use the device steps in section 5; we cannot remotely erase files on your device.

The current game has no developer-operated player account to delete.

7. Security and international transfers

Local data is stored in the application's storage area and protected by the operating system's access controls. Game saves are not separately encrypted by the game and should not be used to store secrets. We limit access to support correspondence and diagnostic reports to the purposes described in this policy. Online services use their protected network connections and security controls. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

Unity, Google and their providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. For transfers subject to data protection restrictions, applicable provider agreements and safeguards govern the processing. Further details are available in the providers' privacy policies linked in section 4.

8. Children's privacy

The current game does not ask players to enter a name, birth date, email address or other personal details to play. It has no player chat, public player profiles or targeted advertising. Technical diagnostics described in section 2 can still be processed when the game is used.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child's personal information has been provided or processed improperly, contact max.omelchenko.pl@gmail.com. We will investigate and delete information or restrict processing as required by applicable law.

9. Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective and last updated date appears at the top of this page. We will communicate material changes through an appropriate notice and obtain new consent where required.
